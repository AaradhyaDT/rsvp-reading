## Slide 7

UTP Cable Parameters
 Attenuation (Insertion Loss - IL ): amount of loss in the signal strength as
it propagates down a wire (negative dB gain)
 Factors: Cable length, frequency, construction and quality of cable, aging and humidity.
 Near End Cross Talk (NEXT): measures the ability of a cable to
reject between pairs of wires at the near end of the circuit.
- NEXT value for a given cable type is usually expressed in dB/m
- Ratio of test voltage applied to a pair to the voltage induced in an
adjacent pair.
 Return Loss: measure of ratio of transmitted power into a cable to amount
of power returned/reflected.
 caused by variations in impedance in the connectors and cable.

## Slide 8

UTP Cable Parameters
 Delay Skew: measure of difference in time between the fastest and slowest
wire pair in a UTP cable
- Critical for systems using all pairs for simultaneous transmission.
 Attenuation-to-Crosstalk Ratio (ARC): difference between IL and NEXT.
- Large value indicates greater bandwidth
- Measurement of the quality of the cable

 Optical fiber refers to the medium and the technology associated with the
transmission of information as light pulses along a glass or plastic strand or
fiber.

source to a destination through a guided fiber cable (glass or plastic) are
called fiber optic system.
 Because of high information carrying capacity and low attenuation, now-a-
days fiber are finding wide application in telecommunications, Local area
networks, sensors, computer networks etc.

Any load impedance can be matched to the line by using single stub
technique. The drawback of this approach is that if the load is changed,
the location of insertion may have to be moved.
The transmission line realizing the stub is normally terminated by a short
or by an open circuit. In many cases it is also convenient to select the same

necessary. The choice of open or shorted stub may depend in practice on a
number of factors. A short circuited stub is less prone to leakage of
electromagnetic radiation and is somewhat easier to realize.
